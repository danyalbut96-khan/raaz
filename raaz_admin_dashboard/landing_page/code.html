Common Questions

Is my identity really anonymous? expand_more

Yes. RAAZ uses end-to-end encryption for all interactions. We don't link your email or phone number to your public profile, and your data is scrubbed of all metadata before it ever reaches other users.

How do you prevent harassment? expand_more

We utilize advanced AI moderation coupled with community reporting to maintain a safe environment. Harassment or hate speech results in an immediate and permanent ban.

Is the app free to use? expand_more

RAAZ is free to download and use. We offer a premium subscription called 'RAAZ+' which includes extra customization, advanced reflection tools, and supports our mission of privacy.
