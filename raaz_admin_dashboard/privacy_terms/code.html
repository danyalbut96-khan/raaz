Privacy Policy Last Updated: October 24, 2023

At RAAZ, we prioritize your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and handle your information when you use our website and mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the absolute minimum data required to provide our services. This includes:

  • Account Credentials: We use cryptographic hashing for passwords; we never store plain-text credentials.
  • Device Metadata: Basic device type and operating system version for technical troubleshooting.
  • Anonymous Contributions: Any content you share is decoupled from your identity using our proprietary masking layer.

2. How We Protect Your Data

Security is not a feature at RAAZ; it is our foundation. We employ several layers of protection:

  1. End-to-end encryption for all peer-to-peer communications.
  2. Zero-knowledge architecture, meaning even our administrators cannot read your private messages.
  3. Regular third-party security audits and penetration testing.

3. Cookies and Tracking

RAAZ does not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We use only essential functional cookies to keep you logged in and maintain your session preferences.

4. Data Retention

We do not keep data longer than necessary. Anonymous posts are stored based on community engagement rules, while account data is deleted immediately upon account termination request.

Questions about your privacy?

Contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@raaz.app for any inquiries regarding your personal information.
