General

RAAZ is an enterprise-grade anonymous communication and data sharing platform designed for organizations that prioritize absolute privacy. We use zero-knowledge architecture to ensure your data stays yours.

Simply download the app, create a local vault, and generate your unique RAAZ identity. No email or phone number is required to sign up, maintaining your complete anonymity from day one.

Privacy

Yes, all data is end-to-end encrypted using AES-256-GCM. We never have access to your private keys, meaning even if our servers were compromised, your data remains unreadable to anyone but you and your recipients.

We have a strict zero-logs policy. We do not track IP addresses, connection timestamps, or session durations. Our system is architected to prioritize metadata obfuscation.

Account

Account recovery is only possible through your 24-word recovery phrase. Because we do not store your identity, we cannot reset your password. Please keep your recovery phrase in a secure, offline location.

Technical

RAAZ is compatible with macOS 12+, Windows 10 (64-bit), and modern Linux distributions. Our mobile app requires iOS 15+ or Android 10+.
