Community Guidelines

RAAZ is built on trust, anonymity, and mutual respect. These guidelines ensure our community remains a safe space for everyone to share their truths.

Posting Rules

Be Authentic

Share your own stories and truths. RAAZ is for human expression, not commercial promotion or spam.

Keep it Legal

Do not post content that encourages illegal acts or shares confidential proprietary information.

  • check_circle
    Anonymity is a Shield, Not a Weapon

    Your identity is protected so you can speak freely, not so you can harass others. We strictly prohibit doxxing.

  • check_circle
    Quality Content

    Low-effort, repetitive, or nonsensical posts may be removed to keep the community feed valuable.

Safety Protocols

error

Zero Tolerance: Harassment

We have a zero-tolerance policy for hate speech, bullying, or targeted harassment. This includes discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or sexual orientation.

Immediate Ban No Warning Permanent
no_photography

Personal Privacy

Never share phone numbers, home addresses, or private photos of others without explicit consent.

health_and_safety

Self-Harm

Posts promoting self-harm will be removed and we will provide resources for professional help immediately.

Identity Masking

Our technology actively scrubs PII (Personally Identifiable Information) from your posts before they go live.

Moderation Process

1

Automated Scanning

AI systems scan content for banned keywords and safety violations in real-time.

2

Community Reporting

Users flag content that breaches guidelines. High-report content is hidden automatically.

3

Human Review

Expert moderators review flagged content to make final decisions on appeals and bans.

See something wrong?

Help us maintain the RAAZ environment. Reporting is anonymous and helps our AI learn faster.
