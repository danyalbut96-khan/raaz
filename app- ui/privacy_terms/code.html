Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2023

At RAAZ, we prioritize your anonymity and the security of your data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our anonymous sharing platform.

1. Data Minimization Principle

We operate on a "zero-knowledge" philosophy wherever technically feasible. We do not require real names, phone numbers, or social media linking to participate in the RAAZ ecosystem.

2. Information Collection

While we strive for absolute anonymity, certain technical data is required to provide the service:

  • Device identifiers (for session management only)
  • IP addresses (temporary storage for DDoS protection, purged every 24 hours)
  • User-generated content (posts, interactions, comments)

3. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to facilitate the delivery of content, maintain platform safety, and improve user experience through aggregated, non-identifiable metrics.

info

Important Security Note

RAAZ employs end-to-end encryption for private communications and utilizes sophisticated hashing for all account-related tokens.

4. Data Retention

We retain user content only as long as necessary. Users may delete their shares at any time, which triggers a permanent purge from our active databases within 72 hours.

5. Contact Information

For any privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your data rights, please reach out to our privacy officer at privacy@raaz.app.
