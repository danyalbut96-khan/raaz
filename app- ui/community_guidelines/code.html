Community Guidelines

RAAZ is a space for authentic connection and honest sharing. To keep our community safe and high-quality, we ask everyone to follow these core principles.

list_alt

The Golden Rules

1

Zero Tolerance for Bullying: We do not allow content that harasses, threatens, or targets individuals for abuse.

2

Protect Identity: Never share personally identifiable information (PII) about yourself or others. Keep it anonymous.

3

No Illegal Content: Content promoting illegal activities or regulated goods will be removed immediately.

shield_with_heart

Safe Posting

Think before you post. While you are anonymous, your words have impact. Ensure your content is helpful, creative, or thoughtfully reflective.

Self-care Privacy first
groups

Respect Others

Diverse opinions are welcome. Disagree respectfully. Hate speech, racism, and discriminatory content have no place in our ecosystem.

Empathy Kindness

Building a better space together.

Our moderation team and community work 24/7 to maintain these standards.
